Privacy, in plain terms.

This website and the iPhone waitlist are separate from the Android app.

The Android app

The current Android app does not require an account. Its complete policy covers Accessibility access, private session storage, website icons, diagnostics you choose to email and optional Google Play purchases.

Android privacy policy ↗

The existing Android policy remains at its original address. This website does not replace it.

This website

There are no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, embedded social feeds or externally loaded fonts on this site. It does not use cookies or browser storage to track you or save a waitlist signup.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like other web hosts, GitHub may process technical request information such as your IP address. Opening Google Play or another external link takes you to that service, where its own policy applies.

The iPhone waitlist

The waitlist is for Clarity’s iPhone launch notifications only. Consent is optional: you can use this site and the Android app without joining.

When the waitlist is open and you submit the form, your email address, your consent, the source “iphone” and a spam-prevention field are sent to the configured waitlist service. Your browser also sends ordinary network information to that service. There is no account or payment involved.

Email addresses are not published on this website or returned in the acknowledgement. A signup is confirmed only after the service accepts it; the page does not save a local-only entry or pretend you joined when the service is unavailable.

The waitlist is operated by Thomas Nicholson on a privately managed server, with Cloudflare handling the public network connection. Email addresses are stored in a private database, not GitHub, and are used only for iPhone launch notifications and handling removal requests. Joining does not send a confirmation email.

The service also stores the first acceptance time, consent and source. For spam prevention, it keeps a keyed, one-way representation of your network address for short-term rate limits, not your raw IP address in the application database. Cloudflare and the hosting infrastructure process ordinary network request information.

Waitlist entries are scheduled for deletion after 365 days from first acceptance; joining again does not extend that period. Cleanup runs periodically and when the service starts, so outages or a cleanup backlog can delay removal. You can request earlier removal using the contact below. We do not sell the list or use it for a general newsletter.

Questions or removal requests

For privacy questions, or to request removal from the iPhone list after joining, email changeengine64@gmail.com. Include the email address you used to join. Do not send passwords or payment details.
