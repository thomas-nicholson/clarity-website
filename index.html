A few useful things to know.

How does blocking work?

After you enable Accessibility access, Clarity checks visible apps and supported browser address bars against the targets in your active sessions. Website blocking is best-effort: browser updates and device settings can affect it. It is a focus aid, not an unbreakable lock.

Do I need an account?

No. The current Android app keeps its focus sessions in private app storage and does not require an account. The optional iPhone waitlist is separate from the app.

Are charges automatic?

No. You can choose no fee, or choose an optional Google Play purchase. Paid options show Google Play’s exact local price before checkout and require you to initiate the purchase. There are no automatic penalty charges.

What about privacy?

The Android privacy policy explains local Accessibility checks, session storage, favicon requests and optional Google Play purchases. Our website privacy page covers this site and the separate iPhone waitlist.
