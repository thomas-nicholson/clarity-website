A little help getting focused.

For setup questions, a problem with blocking, or feedback about Clarity.

Contact

Email changeengine64@gmail.com. If an email app does not open, copy the address into your preferred mail service.

For Android issues, include your Clarity version, Android version, browser name if relevant, and what you expected to happen. Don’t include passwords, payment card details or private browsing content.

Blocking needs Accessibility access

Use the guided setup in Clarity to enable access and make your first session. In Help, you can reopen setup or choose Repair blocking. Browser compatibility and device settings can affect website blocking; it is best-effort.

Taking a break

Use Temporary Unblock when you need a timed pause and full editing. Resume blocking ends the pause early. Optional paid penalties always require a Google Play purchase you initiate; there are no automatic charges.

Purchases and refunds

If Google Play reports a pending purchase, check its status before starting another. Use Clarity’s Help options for purchase support. Never email a purchase token or your card details.

iPhone, browser extensions and Sync

iPhone is not available yet. Chrome and Firefox store downloads and cross-device Sync are coming soon. There is no release date to promise. The iPhone waitlist form reports honestly if signup is not open.

Privacy and waitlist removal

See website and waitlist privacy or the existing Android policy. For removal from the iPhone list after joining, email the support address above using the address you registered.
